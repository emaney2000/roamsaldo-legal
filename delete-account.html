Delete your RoamSaldo account

Updated: September 28, 2026

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open RoamSaldo and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings → Data & Account.
  3. Tap Delete Account and confirm.

You can export your trip data first from the same screen if you would like a copy.

From the web (if the app is no longer installed)

Email us from the address on your account:

roamsaldo.admin@gmail.com
Subject: Account deletion request

We will verify that the request comes from the account owner and confirm by email once the account has been deleted. Please do not include your password in the email — we will never ask for it.

What gets deleted

  • Your RoamSaldo account and sign-in credentials.
  • Trip settings: destination, currencies, budget, dates, and saved rates.
  • Expenses you logged, including notes and categories.
  • Imported card transactions and category overrides.
  • Your card connection — the access token is revoked at Plaid so the connection stops there too.

Live data is removed immediately. Encrypted backups are purged within 90 days. We may keep a minimal record of the deletion request itself where we are required to by law or need it for security and fraud prevention.
